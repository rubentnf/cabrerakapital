## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. Some are necessary for the website to work; others can only be used if you allow them.

## 2. Cookies used on this website

This website **does not use its own advertising or tracking cookies**. Only two items store information on your device:

| Name | Owner | Purpose | Duration | Does it need your permission? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ck-consentimiento | Cabrera Kapital | Remember whether you have accepted or rejected the map cookies. It is stored in the browser's local storage. | 12 months | No, it is technical |
| NID and other Google cookies | Google | Show the office map and remember your Google Maps preferences. | Up to 6 months (some longer if you are signed in to Google) | Yes |

Google cookies **are only set if you accept them** in the cookie notice or press «Show map» on the contact page. If you reject them, the map is not loaded and you can open the location directly in Google Maps with the «Get directions» link shown below it.

You can see how Google uses these cookies at [policies.google.com/technologies/cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## 3. Cookie-free visitor statistics

To find out how many people visit the website we use Umami, an analytics tool that **does not set cookies** or store data in your browser, does not store your full IP address and cannot identify you. It therefore does not need your consent. More details in the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/politica-de-privacidad/).

## 4. How to accept, reject or change your choice

When you first visit the website you will see a notice with two buttons, «Accept» and «Reject». You can change your choice at any time with the **«Cookie settings»** link in the footer of every page.

Your choice is remembered for 12 months; after that you will be asked again.

If you withdraw your permission, the map stops loading, but any cookies Google has already set remain in your browser until they expire or you delete them. You can delete them from your browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
