## 3. Nature of the activity

Cabrera Kapital acts as an independent intermediary and is not contractually tied to any particular financial institution.

Its activity consists of:

- Intermediation in obtaining mortgage financing.
- Personalised analysis of the client's financial profile.
- Presentation of offers from different institutions.
- Individual advice on real estate credit products.
- Negotiation of terms in the client's interest.

Cabrera Kapital does not grant financing directly or act as a credit institution.

## 4. Professional liability insurance

Cabrera Kapital holds Professional Liability Insurance in force, as required by law, with sufficient cover for carrying out its intermediation activity.

This insurance covers any liability arising from its professional practice.

## 5. Remuneration and fees

Cabrera Kapital receives fees exclusively from the client.

It does not receive commissions, incentives or remuneration from financial institutions.

The fees:

- Are only payable in the event of success.
- Success means the signing of the mortgage loan before a notary.
- Will be communicated to the client in advance through an individual contract.
- No advance payments will be required.
